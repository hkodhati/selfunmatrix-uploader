SelfUnMatrix Uploader

A personal tool that uploads lesson videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, !SelfUnMatrix!. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

The uploader uses Google sign-in only to upload videos and read basic details of the channel that signed in (to confirm it is the right channel). It requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared or sent anywhere except Google's own APIs.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person, viewer or account.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or any purpose other than uploading the owner's videos.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
